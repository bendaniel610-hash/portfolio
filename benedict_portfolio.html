Project 03

Trash Compactor Redesign

A product design deep-dive — identified a real UX flaw in the Joseph Joseph Titan compactor and engineered a fix from the ground up.

UX Problem → Engineering Solution

Horizontal removal.
One hand. No strain.

The original design forces users to lift heavy bags upward. Redesigned the front cabinet door with a hole-pin hinge and push-to-open latch for single-hand horizontal bag removal — eliminating the core pain point.
